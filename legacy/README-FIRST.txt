X360Linux - LEGACY XBOX 360 LINUX ARCHIVE (circa 2011)
=================================================

EXPERIMENTAL HISTORICAL PACKAGE - NOT A CURRENT X360LINUX RELEASE.

The files in this ZIP were supplied as a legacy Xbox 360 Linux boot
collection. They are provided as a historical reference. No device test
was performed and compatibility with any Xbox 360 model is NOT guaranteed.

Files:
  default.xex               Legacy XeLLLaunch application
  xell.bin                  Older XeLL loader image
  vmlinux                   PowerPC Linux ELF boot payload
  casper/filesystem.squashfs Historical live Linux root filesystem
  readme.txt                Historical XeLLLaunch instructions

WARNING:
- Requires an Xbox 360 already modified with a compatible RGH/JTAG XeLL
  environment. Does not work on a stock Xbox 360.
- Do not flash this XeLL image or modify the NAND.
- Keep a working recovery method and a backup of your existing setup.
- These binaries were created in 2011; the software may be insecure.
  Do not use it for sensitive browsing, accounts or personal data.
- There is no guarantee that the XeLL version matches your hardware.
- The package is NOT the new Linux 6.18 / xenos-linux-gpu integration.
- Third-party licenses and source provenance require independent review.
